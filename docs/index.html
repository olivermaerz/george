George Is Looking Busy

He looks very busy.

George waits until you actually go idle, then wanders the cursor, clicks only the targets you pick, and pastes the phrases you configure. Option-Shift-Esc if someone walks in.

Pick the spots. Write the lines. Walk away.

Movement is always on once George is armed. Clicks and text stay off until you enable them. Nothing is clicked at random.

Waits until you are idle

George watches for real mouse and keyboard activity, then starts after the timeout you set. Arm it and leave. Or skip the wait and start immediately.

Wanders on a cadence

Steady every N seconds, or random waits between a min and max. The cursor moves because you armed him — not because a screensaver kicked in.

Clicks only where you said

Mark targets on screen. After a run of mouse moves, George clicks one of them — in order or at random among the list you made.

Pastes your phrases

Each text action clicks the spot you marked, pastes a line, and can press Enter. Handy for a recurring command in a terminal you already opened.

Sounds like someone is there

Optional typing bursts, the occasional hmm, a throat-clear. Toggle it from the menu extra without opening settings.

Panic: Option-Shift-Esc

One chord and George disarms. Grant Accessibility once, relaunch, and he is ready. macOS 14 or later.
